About Me

Technology generalist with depth in building and protecting systems.

I'm a computer scientist who works across the full technology stack. I design and develop websites and software, analyse data as an analytical engineer, secure systems as a cyber security practitioner, and keep hardware and networks running as a technician.

I also work as a prompt engineer, crafting instructions that get reliable, high-quality results from AI tools. I enjoy solving practical problems and turning ideas into working products.

Outside tech, I make music with the record label HillsNation: gospel, love ballads and motivational songs, all guided by the message "Hold no grudge."

I am also part of Xander, a development society I'm part of with my Tech Broz, where we build software projects together.
